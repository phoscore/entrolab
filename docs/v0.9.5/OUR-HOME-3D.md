# Our Home: local 3D and model-time trails (Phase 2F-B)

The 2D Solar System view remains available. The optional 3D view projects the unchanged heliocentric J2000 ecliptic X/Y/Z vectors from JPL Table 1 onto a 2D Canvas. Sun at [0,0,0]; Earth's position is the Earth–Moon barycentre. TDB interval: 1800-01-01 through 2050-01-01 inclusive. The same selected body, reference body, model time, relative-velocity and 3D-distance comparison survive switching views. Camera angle, scale and focus alter only projected pixels. The map and numeric measurements do not share a screen-space distance.

The orbit camera keeps an angular yaw/pitch, zoom and a focus point. Its orthonormal projection basis and perspective constants are computed once per Canvas frame, then reused for all orbital and trail points. View presets are top, angled and side. Perspective uses a depth denominator and clips points behind the near plane; bodies are painted far to near. The top pose has X right and Y up, matching the existing 2D coordinate orientation. Drag/arrow keys rotate; pinch, focused wheel, slider and +/- zoom. The scale bar measures AU **at the focus depth**: perspective means its apparent length is not uniform across the picture. Body discs and Saturn's ring are illustrative, and default marker radii are enlarged for visibility. Full system restores the Sun-centred angled camera. This view uses only the heliocentric JPL fit. [Galactic Motion](OUR-HOME-GALACTIC-MOTION.md) adds a separate local frame and model-time flight trails.

Trails are **past positions in the approximate model**, not photographed light paths. For each body the window is 18% of its approximate Keplerian period (365.256 × a^(3/2) TDB days, where a is the existing epoch's model semimajor axis). The window is truncated at the model's 1800 boundary. Stützpunkte are sampled over that time interval from `orbitAt`, `eccentricAnomaly` and `vectorOnOrbit` at the sample TDB epochs; no screenshot coordinates or frame history enter the calculation. The segment count is bounded to 24–96 using at most approximately 1.5° of mean orbital motion per segment, tightened for eccentricity. At the first supported epoch the trail is a single point. A paused frame reuses the same samples when the camera moves. A changed model time recomputes them, including at 100 model days per real second. Samples are projected afresh for each camera angle, faded by temporal age and skipped if behind the near plane. Maximum resident sample count is 8 × 97 = 776 vectors plus small objects. This is a visual sampling rule within the fitted ellipses, not a physical uncertainty interval.

The JPL approximate model and its nominal errors are documented in [Home & Systems](HOME-AND-SYSTEMS.md) and [Our Home Motion](OUR-HOME-MOTION.md). 3D perspective cannot establish distance from apparent visual proximity. Existing Solar numeric comparisons remain computed from the original three-dimensional vectors at one TDB epoch. The site neither introduces additional data nor modifies astronomical parameters.

Verification: `tests/solar-3d.test.mjs` checks vector identity at sampled epochs, projection orientation, depth, boundary clipping, frame independence and timing; `tests/our-home-3d-browser.test.mjs` captures 1440/390/320 px with top/angle/side, touch/keyboard, pause/playback, 100 switches, DE/EN, Reduced Motion and idle.
