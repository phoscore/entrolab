# Source credits for the Cosmic Explorer data sample

**ESA/Gaia/DPAC.** The astrometry originates from ESA’s Gaia mission and processing by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). National institutions fund DPAC, particularly those in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement. Accessed through the German Astrophysical Virtual Observatory. Modified here by selecting columns and rows and normalizing them to the Explorer data contract. Cite [Gaia mission (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629272) and [Gaia DR3 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243940). [ESA credit and use conditions](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Miscellaneous/sec_credit_and_citation_instructions/).

**C. A. L. Bailer-Jones, J. Rybizki, M. Fouesneau, M. Demleitner and R. Andrae.** Geometric and photogeometric Gaia EDR3 distance estimates, I/352, joined through GAVO. [Bailer-Jones et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/abd806) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [GAVO metadata](https://dc.g-vo.org/gedr3dist/q/cone/info). Modified here by selecting and joining rows, renaming fields and preserving the published quantiles in parsecs.

**Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) Collaboration.** Six DR1 `iron` zpix rows from NOIRLab Astro Data Lab. Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory manages DESI construction and operations. Support comes from the U.S. DOE Office of Science, Office of High-Energy Physics (DE-AC02-05CH11231), and NERSC, a DOE user facility under that contract. Further support comes from NSF’s Division of Astronomical Sciences (AST-0950945 to NSF NOIRLab), UK STFC, the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation, the Heising-Simons Foundation, France’s CEA, Mexico’s CONAHCYT, Spain’s MICINN and the DESI member institutions. DESI acknowledges permission to conduct research on I'oligam Du'ag (Kitt Peak), which has particular significance to the Tohono O’odham Nation. Authors’ conclusions do not necessarily represent the funders’ views. Modified here by selecting a narrow range of primary bright Main Survey galaxy rows, selecting columns and normalizing their encoding. [DR1 and papers](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/dr1/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [Official DESI acknowledgment and citation text for downstream use](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/acknowledgments/).

**Open Exoplanet Catalogue, Hanno Rein and contributors.** Three selected planet records projected from pinned XML into JSON, omitting prose, media and unused measurements. The original project’s database license and notice accompany this sample in [OEC-LICENSE.txt](OEC-LICENSE.txt). [Catalogue](https://github.com/OpenExoplanetCatalogue/open_exoplanet_catalogue/tree/4a61419b92d4a57366b111b2d11d0a03eb0b5df0) · [Rein (2012)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.7121).
