# Source credits for the Cosmic Explorer data sample

**ESA/Gaia/DPAC.** The astrometry originates from ESA’s Gaia mission and processing by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC). National institutions fund DPAC, particularly those in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement. Accessed through the German Astrophysical Virtual Observatory. Modified here by selecting columns and rows and normalizing them to the Explorer data contract. Cite [Gaia mission (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629272) and [Gaia DR3 (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243940). [ESA credit and use conditions](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Miscellaneous/sec_credit_and_citation_instructions/).

**C. A. L. Bailer-Jones, J. Rybizki, M. Fouesneau, M. Demleitner and R. Andrae.** Geometric and photogeometric Gaia EDR3 distance estimates, I/352, joined through GAVO. [Bailer-Jones et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/abd806) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [GAVO metadata](https://dc.g-vo.org/gedr3dist/q/cone/info). Modified here by selecting and joining rows, renaming fields and preserving the published quantiles in parsecs.

**Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) Collaboration.** Six DR1 `iron` zpix rows obtained from NOIRLab Astro Data Lab. Modified here by selecting a narrow range of primary bright Main Survey galaxy rows, selecting columns and normalizing their encoding. [DESI Collaboration et al. (2026), Data Release 1](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ae4c43) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) · [DESI license and acknowledgments](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/acknowledgments/).
