# Real Sky — Gaia DR3 bright-source selection

Data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission [Gaia](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/gaia), processed by the [Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC)](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dpac/consortium). DPAC funding comes from national institutions, including participants in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement. The catalogue is credited to ESA/Gaia/DPAC. The captured CSV selection uses the [GAVO Data Center Gaia DR3 Lite](https://dc.g-vo.org/browse/gaia/q3) mirror. The original-row cross-check uses the ESA Gaia Archive.

- Gaia Collaboration et al. (2016), *The Gaia mission*, [A&A 595, A1](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629272).
- Gaia Collaboration et al. (2023), *Gaia Data Release 3: Summary of the content and survey properties*, [A&A 674, A1](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243940).
- [ESA Gaia DR3 acknowledgement and references](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Miscellaneous/sec_credit_and_citation_instructions/).
- [GAVO catalogue description and rights statement](https://dc.g-vo.org/gaia/q3/cone/howtocite).

This is a small, attributed selection of published numerical catalogue facts for educational visualisation. It retains G ≤ 7.8, valid sky coordinates and solution types 3, 31 or 95. Detail pages contain the original selected CSV rows. The spatial index and aggregate counts are derived for this map. Colours represent selected catalogue counts or measured G brightness bands. The selection does not establish complete coverage, absolute star density, classification as a star or distance. ICRS catalogue directions are fixed at J2016.0 (TCB). The data credits apply independently of the viewer source-code terms.
